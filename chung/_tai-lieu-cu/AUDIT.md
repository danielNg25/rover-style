## 2. Cross-cutting problems

These recur across shots and should be fixed once in shared code or shared art. Each item only groups findings confirmed in section 3.

1. **Post blur / depth of field where the reference is crisp flat vector.** Shots: hall-back (blur 0.35), alcove (0.25), village (mean gradient about 25% lower), ocean (0.12), hall-raise (0.55), hall-wide (backdrop), hall-globe, closeup (mask edges softer). Fix: set Post blur to 0 per shot. Exception: maskcase, where the skeptic refuted the blur claim (leave the blur amount alone).
2. **Bloom / glow halos on lanterns and fires.** Shots: hall-back, hall-raise, hall-wide, hall-globe (lantern halos, lit lantern), alcove (near-white brazier halo over the arch), village (lit yellow lantern pane), hall-raise (stairs blown out to near white by the hand glow from the first frame). Reference lantern panes are flat cream with no halo.
3. **Four-point flare / glint sprites that the reference does not have.** Shots: maskcase (4 case glints, 5 sky flares), alcove (4 ring glints), hall-back (flare stars), hall-raise (ball sparkle, sky star), hall-wide (ball glint at 419), hall-globe (ball sparkle at 538), savanna (diamond glints over animals, canopy and sky). Remove the shared Flare/glint use in these shots.
4. **Ambient floating dust specks that the reference does not have.** Shots: hall-back, closeup, king, village, hall-wide, alcove (large soft blobs on the navy), savanna (too big, too many). hall-raise specks were seen but not compared. Disable the shared speck layer per shot.
5. **Shared Doom character art (doomImg parts).** Recurring in maskcase, alcove, closeup, king, village, hall-back, hall-raise, hall-wide, hall-globe:
   - mask is egg/oval or squat instead of a rounded square with near-vertical sides; lighter neutral grey (#b2b2b2-#b7b7b7) instead of blue-grey (#989ea3-#9aa0a8); pupils too large and yellow (maskcase, closeup, hall-raise, hall-wide, hall-globe; king: cool grey vs warm cream);
   - no near-black hood interior band around the mask (closeup, hall-raise, hall-wide, hall-globe);
   - scalloped / layered mantle and cape instead of a flat panel with straight edges; cape too wide (closeup, hall-raise, hall-globe, king, village back, alcove cape silhouette, hall-back hood shows collar and cape);
   - costume green too light and saturated (alcove #31733c vs #275d32, closeup #52a262 vs #2c6b3a, hall-raise #327c3b vs #276332);
   - clenched fist where the reference has an open hand (closeup, hall-globe, village); segmented / banded arms instead of one smooth forearm (king, hall-raise, hall-globe); hands too large (hall-raise);
   - magenta-purple fringe on sprite edges (closeup, king).
6. **Character acting timing and cadence.** Reference characters move on twos with staged in-betweens; ours move on ones or swap pose in one frame. Shots: closeup (lean-in, tip shift), village (figure on ones, villagers swap in one frame), hall-raise (on ones, 18 px pop in one frame), hall-globe (30-40 px head snap at 498-499), king (arm pops, no chest/belly travel poses). Extra blinks or closed eyes not in the reference: king (178-180, 203-205), village (5 blinks), hall-wide (413, 437, 442-445). Extra gaze beats: hall-raise (394-397), hall-globe (533).
7. **Star fields and space backdrops.** Reference stars are sparse, crisp squares/diamonds at constant brightness. Shots: earth (grey halo discs, dust 2.7x too dense and uniform, nebula too light), hall-back (blurred round blobs, flares, twinkle, stars over the cloud), maskcase (larger, pulsing), alcove windows (soft, twinkling, 11 vs 7-9), sun (350-380 dust specks vs about 150, bright-star count not steady, bluer background), hall-raise (four-point star), hall-wide (no stars at all in the checked patch). Density must be handled per shot: the hall-back "double the count" claim was refuted.
8. **Throne hall backdrop and brightness (shared ThroneHall set).** Shots: hall-back, hall-raise, hall-wide, hall-globe. Lavender towers / snow / pines / pale wall instead of dark navy sky with green and teal hills, clouds and slim towers; floor and steps too bright; frame too bright (hall-back +11%, hall-wide luma 171.5 vs 151-153.5, hall-globe about +8 levels).
9. **Throne hall props (shared ThroneHall set).** Diamond panels are an even grid instead of 3 rows of 2+2 pairs (hall-back, hall-raise, hall-wide, hall-globe); big throne top (pediment, disc, spires, beam) missing or out of frame (hall-back, hall-wide); small throne too small / misplaced / missing green cushion and runner (hall-back, hall-raise, hall-wide, hall-globe); left lantern overlaps the armillary (hall-back, hall-raise, hall-globe); armillary has thick extra rings, a spinning globe and fast beads (hall-back, hall-wide; rings too thick in hall-raise, hall-globe); foreground columns too wide, misplaced or with extra blocks (hall-back, hall-raise, hall-wide, hall-globe); faceted gold balls (hall-wide, maskcase).
10. **Camera: wrong kind of move.** hall-back (pull-back instead of a small truck), hall-wide (push-in and head-on view instead of an off-axis lateral truck), ocean (no pan, reference pans about 30 px), king (all layers slide together, 2% zoom, eased instead of linear), village (no pull-back at ground and villager depth), earth (backdrop pan missing), hall-raise (character grows x1.20 instead of x1.05-1.10).
11. **Flames (shared flame code in bkit).** maskcase and alcove: rows of straight rigid spikes instead of one ragged mass / 3 fat tongues that change outline; coal bed is a flat band with evenly spaced dots.
12. **Two-frame hash stepping reads as strobing.** sun: surface granule sphere and corona flames both re-randomise on floor(f/2); prominence loops also pulse.
13. **Local colour off while whole-frame exposure matches.** maskcase (wall, floor, left column darker), alcove (wainscot, floor), closeup (panel greener, vignette darkens corners), king (walls brighter, throne golds and reds), savanna (lower third about +20 in R/G, greens too saturated), ocean (near water lighter, sky one flat blue), sun (corona and halo too bright, +13% luma).
14. **Shaded / rounded art where the reference is flat cut-paper.** king (king design), village (six villagers), savanna (elephants, giraffes, acacias), ocean (dolphins read as sharks), earth (saucer clouds).
15. **Stray shadows and leftover geometry.** maskcase (saw-toothed shadow on wall panel), king (jagged floor shadow, grey bar, cast shadow on pillar), closeup (wall step at lower left), village (dark smear on plinth, shadow in the wrong place), hall-wide (green seat slab beside Doom).

## 5. Coverage statement

What this audit did:
- Compared the reference and our render (`out/full.mp4`, 540 frames, 0-539) for 13 shots covering every film frame.
- Per shot, one auditor scored the shot and listed differences; one independent skeptic then re-checked every claim, confirmed, corrected, refuted or added findings, and re-scored. All 13 shots have a skeptic verdict.
- The evidence is still images only: extracted full-resolution frames at selected frame numbers, cropped and zoomed stills, and frame strips (rows of frames at step 1 to step 11) stored under `out/audit/<shot>/`.
- Numeric checks were made on extracted frames: pixel colour samples and region means, bounding boxes and row/column scans, blob counts, per-frame position tracking of selected features, frame-to-frame difference means, and ffmpeg signalstats.

What this audit did not do:
- No one in this audit watched either video in motion. Every statement about motion, timing, flicker, twinkle or strobing is inferred from frame strips, per-frame measurements and frame differences, not from playback.
- Audio was not checked for any shot.
- Many frames were seen only in downscaled strips or at sampled steps (2 to 11 frames); timings from those are accurate to about the step size. Each shot's "Not verified" list states the gaps.
- Some generated strips were never opened (earth v-end.jpg; maskcase v-flameR.jpg, v-sparkR.jpg, v-edge.jpg; alcove v-winR.jpg; closeup v-tr.jpg, v-edge.jpg; king v-head.jpg).
- Several source files were not read (`src/shots/bkit.tsx`, `src/chars/doomImg.ts`, most of `src/HallSeq.tsx`), so fix hints that name code values are taken from the auditors' notes and are not all cross-checked against source.
- Many positions, sizes, counts and hex values are by-eye estimates (noted per shot); they are targets to re-measure after fixing, not exact specifications.
- The cut after frame 539 could not be compared because our file ends there.
- Nothing was re-rendered and no fix was applied or tested. The writer of this report did not open any image or video; it only consolidated the auditors' and skeptics' written verdicts and added no findings of its own.
